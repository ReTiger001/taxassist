税务智能知识助手

一个本地运行的税务政策检索库：把国家税务总局与 31 个省级税务局的 政策文件收进本机，判定每份文件的现行效力，并让全文可检索。

它解决什么问题

查政策不难，难的是三件事：文件散在 32 个站点、不知道哪份还有效、 附件里的正文搜不到。本站针对这三件事：

  • 一处检索 —— 标题、文号、正文一起搜，支持按地区、栏目、效力、年份筛。
  • 效力判定且依据可溯 —— 每条效力结论都写明依据（官方标注 / 据他文判定 / 未发现废止）， 依据原文可在详情页看到。
  • 附件正文可检索 —— PDF、Word、Excel、压缩包里的正文都解析入库； 老式 .doc/.wps 通过本机 WPS 转换后解析。

数据边界

全部政策原文、译文、判定与附件都保存在本机，不上传任何第三方。 服务默认只监听 127.0.0.1；需要外部访问时经自建隧道（HTTPS）。

必须说清的局限

  • 「未发现废止」不等于「官方确认有效」。它表示库内没有任何文件说它废止。 政策库不可能收全所有废止公告，重要事项请核对原文。
  • 施行日期覆盖约三分之一。多数政策正文确实没写具体施行日期，这一项已接近原文可提取的上限。
  • 译文为机器翻译，仅供理解参考，不具备法律效力。

政策效力等级

  • 官方标注 政策原文自带时效标注，或官方废止目录点名 —— 最硬的一档。
  • 据他文判定 另一份文件写明废止了它，依据可查。
  • 未发现废止 库内无废止证据；不等于官方确认有效。

Tax Policy Knowledge Assistant

A locally-run searchable database of Chinese tax policy: it collects documents from the State Taxation Administration and all 31 provincial tax bureaus, determines whether each document is still in force, and makes the full text searchable.

The problem it solves

Finding a policy is easy. Three things are not: documents are scattered across 32 sites, you cannot tell which are still valid, and text buried in attachments is invisible to search.

  • One place to search — titles, document numbers and body text together, filterable by region, column, validity and year.
  • Validity with traceable evidence — every determination states its basis (official marking / inferred from another document / no repeal found), and the source text is viewable on the detail page.
  • Attachments are searchable — text inside PDF, Word, Excel and archives is parsed and indexed; legacy .doc/.wps files are converted via local WPS.

Data boundary

All policy texts, translations, determinations and attachments are stored on this machine only and never uploaded to any third party. The service binds to 127.0.0.1 by default; external access goes through a self-hosted tunnel over HTTPS.

Limitations we must state

  • "No repeal found" does not mean "officially confirmed valid". It means no document in this database repeals it. No policy database captures every repeal notice — verify against the original text for anything consequential.
  • Effective dates cover about one third. Most policies genuinely do not state a specific effective date; this is close to the extraction ceiling.
  • Translations are machine-generated, for comprehension only, with no legal force.

Validity levels

  • Official — the document itself carries a validity marking, or an official repeal catalogue names it. Strongest.
  • Inferred — another document states it is repealed; the evidence is traceable.
  • No repeal found — no repeal evidence in this database; not an official confirmation of validity.
