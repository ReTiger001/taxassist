服务与价格

数据边界（先读这一条）

库里的全部内容是公开政策文件，来自各级税务局官网，不含任何客户资料。 你在使用中提供的业务事实（合同、方案、想法）只用于当次分析， 不写入政策库，也不发送给任何第三方：

必须说清的局限

下面这些不是免责套话，是实际使用中真的会碰到的边界：

  • 「未发现废止」不等于「官方确认有效」。 它只表示库内没有任何文件说它废止。任何政策库都收不全所有废止公告， 重要事项请核对原文。
  • 施行日期覆盖约六分之一（实测 1,939 / 13,717 ≈ 14%）。 多数政策正文本身就没写施行日期，这一项已接近原文可提取的上限。
  • 译文为机器翻译，仅供理解参考，不具备法律效力。
  • 收录有边界：覆盖总局与 29 个省级行政区（30 余个来源，历史全量 自 1984 年起）。少数栏目是无静态列表的 JS 检索页，暂未接入； 抓取每日运行，漏抓会告警并补抓，但不承诺 100% 收全。
  • 不构成专业意见。本服务提供的是政策资料的检索、整理与效力线索， 不构成税务、法律或投资意见。任何对外出具的结论，都应由执业 人员核对原文后自行作出专业判断。

这一页写的是主线规则；完整条款（含服务范围、可用性与责任边界） 见随合同提供的《服务说明与免责》。

Data boundary (read this first)

Everything in the database is public policy documents from official tax authority websites — no client material of any kind. Business facts you supply (a contract, a plan, an idea) are used for that single analysis and are never written to the database and never sent to a third party:

Limitations we must state

These are not boilerplate — they are boundaries you will actually meet in use:

  • "No repeal found" does not mean "officially confirmed valid". It means no document in this database repeals it. No policy database captures every repeal notice — verify against the original text for anything consequential.
  • Effective dates cover about one sixth (measured 1,939 / 13,717 ≈ 14%). Most policies genuinely do not state one; this is close to the extraction ceiling.
  • Translations are machine-generated, for comprehension only, with no legal force.
  • Coverage has edges: the central administration plus 29 provincial regions (30+ sources, full history back to 1984). A few columns are JS-driven search pages with no static listing and are not yet connected. Collection runs daily and missed pages raise an alert and are re-fetched, but complete coverage is not guaranteed.
  • Not professional advice. This service provides retrieval, organisation and validity leads for policy material. It does not constitute tax, legal or investment advice. Any conclusion issued externally should be reached by a qualified professional against the original text.
